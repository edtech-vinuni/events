16 November – 12 December 2026 · Hanoi, Vietnam

Research. Collaborate. Experience Vietnam.

A four-week research-based mobility program where UWA and VinUniversity students work on AI and cybersecurity projects alongside Vietnamese language and cultural activities.

View program Explore research tracks

Program details are indicative and may be updated as planning is finalised.

Program overview

A research-led international experience

Students work individually or in small teams on AI-related projects. The program combines an introductory session, independent research, mentoring and regular progress discussions.

Students define a research question, review relevant work, develop an appropriate method, evaluate their results and present their findings.

Program at a glance

  • Independent and collaborative research
  • Collaboration with VinUniversity students
  • Vietnamese language classes
  • Industry visits and cultural activities
  • Final research presentations
Research

Four research tracks

Students may select from suggested topics, adapt or combine existing ideas, or propose another relevant topic. Project scope and activities may vary depending on students' backgrounds, interests, available resources and research progress.

01

Trustworthy & Multilingual AI

  • CJKV reasoning and factuality benchmark
  • Vietnamese hallucination and factuality evaluation
  • Vietnamese negation, exceptions and logical reasoning
  • Cultural and social reasoning in Vietnamese
  • Confidence calibration across Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese
  • Evidence-grounded question answering in Vietnamese
02

AI/LLM Security & Trustworthy Cyber Reasoning

  • Veracity validation for LLM-generated cyberattack narratives
  • Evidence-grounded cyber incident reconstruction
  • Hallucination detection in cybersecurity reports
  • Consistency checking for attack narratives
  • Uncertainty-aware cyber investigation assistants
  • Provenance and audit trails for LLM-based cyber analysis
  • Vietnamese or multilingual prompt injection benchmarks
  • Safety checking before an AI agent executes an action
03

Vietnamese Language Data & EdTech

  • Vietnamese learner error corpus
  • Vietnamese pronunciation and tone dataset
  • Vietnamese code-switching dataset
  • Vietnamese pronouns, social hierarchy and forms of address
  • CJKV parallel cultural corpus
  • Vietnamese navigation and everyday instruction dataset
  • AI-assisted feedback for Vietnamese language learners
  • Evaluation of LLMs as Vietnamese language tutors
04

Digital Twin, Cybersecurity & Privacy

  • Cyber digital twin of a university campus
  • Attack-graph-based security simulation
  • Moving Target Defense simulation
  • Digital twin for testing AI agent security
  • Privacy-preserving Vietnamese language learning
  • Federated learning across universities or countries
  • Privacy and security evaluation of educational AI systems
Beyond the classroom

Learning beyond the classroom

Alongside research, students take part in Vietnamese language classes, collaborate with VinUniversity students, and join cultural activities in Hanoi and northern Vietnam.
